Our Story

A Family's Passion, Served with Love

Rhonda Abercrombie's vision was to combine her family's passion for cooking with her love of people and community. The 4 Bistro and Wine Bar is affectionately named in honor of Rhonda's four children — a tribute to the family bonds that inspire every dish we serve.

We bring you delicious family recipes alongside a curated selection of wines and handcrafted cocktails. From our kitchen to your table, every plate is prepared with the warmth and care of a home-cooked meal.

Step inside and you'll find striking decor that enhances the dining experience and fosters a sense of belonging. Whether you're dining on our charming brick-stone patio or enjoying the vibrant atmosphere indoors, you're part of our family.
