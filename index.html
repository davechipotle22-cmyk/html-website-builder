Our Story

Where Family & Flavor Come Together

Rhonda Abercrombie's vision was to combine her family's passion for cooking with her love of people and community.

The Four Bistro and Wine Bar is affectionately named in honor of Rhonda's four children, a tribute to the family bonds that inspire every dish we serve. We bring you delicious family recipes alongside a curated selection of wines and handcrafted cocktails.

Step inside our industrial-chic space with Edison bulb chandeliers, striking decor, and a warm atmosphere that makes everyone feel like family. Whether you're at the bar, a table, or our charming brick-stone patio, you belong here.
